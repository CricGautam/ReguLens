## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes access-control requirements to protect personal data processed by NovaCart's e-commerce platform and supporting business systems.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors, administrators and third-party personnel who access NovaCart systems containing customer personal data.

Covered systems include customer databases, order management systems, customer support platforms and administrative dashboards.

## 3. Access Control Requirements

### 3.1 Role-Based Access Control

**Control ID: NC-AC-001**

Access to customer personal data must be assigned according to documented job responsibilities and the principle of least privilege.

Employees must not receive permissions beyond those required for their assigned duties.

### 3.2 Access Approval

**Control ID: NC-AC-002**

All new access requests must be approved by the relevant department manager and system owner before access is granted.

Approval records must be retained for audit purposes.

### 3.3 Periodic Access Reviews

**Control ID: NC-AC-003**

System owners must conduct access reviews at least once every quarter for systems containing customer personal data.

Reviews must identify unnecessary permissions, inactive accounts and inappropriate privileged access.

Each review must be documented and approved by the responsible system owner.

### 3.4 Privileged Access

**Control ID: NC-AC-004**

Administrative access must be restricted to authorized personnel.

Privileged accounts must use multi-factor authentication where supported and must not be shared between users.

### 3.5 Access Revocation

**Control ID: NC-AC-005**

Access for terminated employees must be disabled within 24 hours of receiving confirmed termination notification.

Access for employees changing roles must be reassessed and adjusted according to their new responsibilities.

### 3.6 Access Monitoring

**Control ID: NC-AC-006**

Access to systems containing customer personal data must be logged.

Security personnel must investigate suspicious access activity according to the organization's incident management procedures.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Information Security Manager | Oversees access-control compliance |
| Department Managers | Approve business access requests |
| System Owners | Review and authorize system access |
| IT Administrators | Implement approved access changes |
| Internal Audit Team | Reviews control design and operating evidence |

## 5. Exceptions

Any exception to this policy must be documented, risk-assessed and approved by the Information Security Manager.

Exceptions must include an expiry date and compensating controls where applicable.

## 6. Compliance Monitoring

NovaCart's Information Security team must periodically assess compliance with this policy.

Missing access-review records, unauthorized privileges and delayed account revocation must be investigated and documented.
